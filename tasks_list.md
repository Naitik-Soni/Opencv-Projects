Here are some **mini project ideas** and **tasks** in **computer vision**, organized by difficulty and focus area. These are great for learning and building a portfolio.

---

## 🔰 **Beginner-Level Tasks**

### 1. **Basic Image Filters**

* **Objective**: Implement Gaussian blur, edge detection (Sobel, Canny), sharpening.
* **Tools**: OpenCV, NumPy.
* **Outcome**: Learn image convolution and basic preprocessing.

### 2. **Color Detection and Tracking**

* **Objective**: Detect and track objects of a specific color in real time (e.g., red ball).
* **Tools**: OpenCV.
* **Skills**: Color spaces (BGR to HSV), masking, contour detection.

### 3. **Face Detection**

* **Objective**: Use Haar cascades or OpenCV’s DNN to detect faces in images/video.
* **Stretch Goal**: Add smile or eye detection.

---

## 🧠 **Intermediate-Level Projects**

### 4. **Object Detection with YOLOv5/YOLOv8**

* **Objective**: Use pretrained models to detect multiple object classes in images.
* **Stretch Goal**: Fine-tune on a custom dataset (e.g., tools, food items).
* **Tools**: Ultralytics YOLO, PyTorch, OpenCV.

### 5. **Image Stitching**

* **Objective**: Merge two overlapping images into a panorama.
* **Concepts**: Feature matching (ORB/SIFT), homography estimation.
* **Tools**: OpenCV.

### 6. **License Plate Detection and OCR**

* **Objective**: Detect license plates and extract the text.
* **Tools**: OpenCV, Tesseract OCR.
* **Challenges**: Dealing with different fonts, angles, and lighting.

### 7. **Gesture Recognition with Hand Tracking**

* **Objective**: Detect hand gestures for simple commands (e.g., volume control).
* **Tools**: MediaPipe Hands, OpenCV.
* **Bonus**: Build a Python app that maps gestures to system commands.

---

## 🚀 **Advanced Projects**

### 8. **Face Recognition System**

* **Objective**: Identify people from a set of known faces.
* **Tools**: `face_recognition` library, Dlib, OpenCV.
* **Stretch Goal**: Add login/authentication functionality.

### 9. **Visual SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping)**

* **Objective**: Build a basic monocular SLAM system.
* **Tools**: OpenCV + Python/C++, ORB-SLAM2.
* **Note**: Deep understanding of geometry and camera models required.

### 10. **Scene Text Detection and Translation (AR-like App)**

* **Objective**: Detect text in the real world, extract it, and translate it.
* **Tools**: OpenCV, Tesseract, Google Translate API.
* **Use Case**: Travel companion for translating signs/menus.

---

## 🧪 **Fun Experiments**

### 11. **Cartoonify Your Image**

* **Objective**: Convert a real photo into a cartoon-style image.
* **Concepts**: Bilateral filtering, edge masking.
* **Tools**: OpenCV, image filters.

### 12. **Real-Time Background Replacement**

* **Objective**: Replace webcam background without a green screen.
* **Tools**: MediaPipe segmentation, OpenCV.
* **Use Case**: Zoom-like background effects.
